Our History
Who We Are

Troop 84G is an all-girl Scouts BSA troop based in North Wales, PA, founded in 2024. We are part of the national Scouts BSA program offered by Scouting America — providing the full Scouts BSA experience for young women, including the complete path to Eagle Scout.

Troop Timeline

2018
Scouting America announces girls may join the Scouts BSA program, launching all-girl troops nationwide beginning February 2019.
2019
Scouts BSA officially opens to girls. All-girl troops form across the country under the same program, ranks, and Eagle Scout requirements.
2024
Troop 84G is officially chartered in North Wales, PA — giving young women in our community their own dedicated Scouts BSA home.
Today
Troop 84G actively meets, camps, earns merit badges, and serves North Wales — with Eagle Scout as our highest aspiration.

Why Scouts BSA?

Scouts BSA® is the traditional Scouting experience — the program that has shaped generations of American leaders since 1910. It's now fully open to girls, with the exact same ranks, merit badges, and Eagle Scout award.

Every girl in Troop 84G has the same opportunity as any Scout in America to achieve Eagle Scout — one of the nation's most recognized youth honors.

Our program is youth-led: scouts plan their own meetings, campouts, and service projects. Adults guide and support — scouts lead.

Join Troop 84G

We welcome girls of all backgrounds and experience levels. No prior scouting experience needed — just curiosity and the drive to grow.

Requirements & How to Enroll
Getting Started Is Easy

Who Can Join

  • Girls ages 11–17 (or 10 years old if they are in 5th grade and join on/after March 1st, OR have earned the Arrow of Life rank)
  • No prior Scouts BSA experience required
  • Girls of all backgrounds and abilities are welcome
  • Special needs accommodated per Scouting America standards

How to Register

  • Attend a Troop 84G meeting
  • Apply Online
  • Email us to connect before your first meeting

For Parents & Volunteers

  • Parents are encouraged to volunteer as adult leaders
  • Background check required for all adult volunteers
  • Free online Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT) required

When We Meet

  • Wednesdays, 7:00 – 8:30pm
  • Full troop meetings: 1st Wednesday · 7:00–8:30pm
  • Patrol meetings: 2nd & 3rd Wednesdays (times vary)
  • Location: North Wales Scout Center, 300 Church St.
  • Schedule subject to change — contact us to confirm
